Method. Propagation timing from a Yellowstone Geyser slot-status stream on a Frankfurt node (SLOT_FIRST_SHRED_RECEIVED→SLOT_COMPLETED, delivery-latency-free). Jito tips summed from tip-account balance deltas; leader fees from block-meta Fee rewards; operators clustered by vote-withdrawer + website. The 6× value is the Ethereum study's Channel C ported over: Δ = transit × (1 − 1/6) of extra sealing delay × the pooled delay slope, per leader slot. Caveats: the delay slopes may still be noisy — if the true slope is zero, so is this; no deadline/headroom cap yet (would lower figures); assumes leaders re-tune sealing to spend the budget; SOL @ $150. Inflation's latency-movable vote-credit slice is not included.
